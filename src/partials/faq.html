Frequently Asked Questions

What does Core9 do?

Core9 develops artificial intelligence technologies that analyze video and operational data to help organizations monitor physical operations across fleets, warehouses, and industrial environments. These systems detect operational risks, identify important events, and generate insights that improve visibility across supply chain operations.

How does Core9 technology work?

Core9 products connect to cameras, telematics devices, and operational sensors across an organization’s physical infrastructure. Artificial intelligence models analyze these data streams continuously to detect events, identify anomalies, and generate insights that help teams understand and manage operational activity.

What types of operations can Core9 monitor?

Core9 products can monitor fleet operations, industrial environments, warehouses, and distribution centers. By analyzing video and operational data, the system provides insights into how physical operations are functioning in real time.

Does Core9 require new infrastructure?

Core9 products can connect to existing cameras, telematics devices, and operational sensors. This allows organizations to deploy AI-based monitoring and analytics without replacing their entire hardware infrastructure.

What insights can organizations gain from Core9 systems?

Core9 products generate insights into driver behavior, operational risks, facility activity, and supply chain operations. These insights help organizations understand patterns, detect issues earlier, and improve operational performance.

How quickly can Core9 be deployed?

Initial deployments can typically be completed within a few weeks depending on the scale of the environment being monitored. Many organizations begin with pilot deployments before expanding across fleets or facilities.
