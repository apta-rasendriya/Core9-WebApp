The problem

Most operations have cameras, dashcams and store photos. When someone asks for proof, the answer is still a footage search. Footage is not evidence.

Scattered

Video, trip logs and photos sit in separate systems, with no single record per incident.

Unexplained

Black-box alerts are hard to defend when an insurer or regulator asks why something was flagged.

Exposed

Continuous recording and cross-border storage create privacy risk under the data laws where you operate.

Data & governance

Four commitments on every Core9 product.

The same rules apply whether the data comes from a truck, a warehouse camera or a store shelf.

Data stays in-country

Video, trip data and store images are processed and stored inside the country where they were captured, within your residency requirements.

Retention you set

We keep event clips and records, not continuous recording. Retention windows are yours to set.

Access you control

Role-based access per site, depot, zone, chain or region, with a log of who viewed what.

Decisions you can explain

Deterministic rules and scoring, so every alert can be explained and reproduced during a claim or audit.

Where we operate

Built to work inside local data laws.
