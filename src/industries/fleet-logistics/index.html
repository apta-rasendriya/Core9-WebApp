01Suggested scope

  • Long-haul and night routes (fatigue and microsleep)
  • Urban delivery routes (phone use and distraction)
  • All pilot vehicles (seatbelt, harsh driving, overspeed)
  • Depots and customer sites (geofences and idling)

02What we set up

  • Driver-facing and road-facing cameras on your existing vehicles
  • Alert thresholds per route type and speed band
  • Escalation from driver to fleet manager to safety lead
  • Driver scoring and a weekly fuel and risk report

03How we measure success

  • Fatigue and distraction events per 1,000 km, before and after
  • Fuel used per route against the pre-pilot baseline
  • Share of alerts acknowledged by drivers in real time
  • Claims and incidents with a complete video record
