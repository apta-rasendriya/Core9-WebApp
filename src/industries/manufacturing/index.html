Compliance

Built for the rules you answer to.

Manufacturing teams across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Pakistan and Indonesia work under specific obligations. CamCore gives you the evidence to show they are met.

KSAWorkplace safety

MHRSD Occupational Safety & Health rules

Continuous PPE and hazard-zone checks, plus the summer midday outdoor work ban (12:00–15:00, 15 June–15 September), with a timestamped record for every event.

UAEWorkplace safety

Ministerial Resolution No. 44 of 2022 on OSH

Evidence that PPE and safe-zone rules are enforced on every shift, and that the midday break (12:30–15:00, 15 June–15 September) is observed.

OmanWorkplace safety

Ministerial Decision No. 286/2008 on OSH

Documented, continuous checks on PPE and restricted areas, and visibility of outdoor work during the summer midday ban.

PakistanWorkplace safety

Punjab OSH Act 2019 · Sindh OSH Act 2017

A searchable record of hazards, near-misses and corrective actions to support the risk assessments both Acts require.

IndonesiaWorkplace safety

SMK3 (PP No. 50/2012) · Law No. 1/1970

The documented, verifiable safety records SMK3 audits expect, captured as work happens instead of compiled before the audit.

Manufacturing · Pilot

Your pilot.

Prove guarding, PPE and forklift detection on one line or plant area, on your existing cameras, with evidence your HSE team and auditors will accept.

01Suggested scope

  • Presses, robots and conveyors (guarded zones)
  • Stations with specific PPE requirements
  • Permit areas used during maintenance
  • Where production meets storage (forklift proximity)

02What we set up

  • Connection to the cameras already covering your lines
  • Rules per station and zone, tied to shift patterns
  • Alerts to line supervisors, with escalation to HSE
  • Shift and line compliance scoring

03How we measure success

  • Guarding and PPE events by line and shift
  • Time from event to supervisor response
  • Compliance gap between day and night shifts
  • Audit-ready records for every event
