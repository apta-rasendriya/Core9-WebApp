Assumptions behind this estimate
  • Recovered sales = your brand's sales × out-of-stock rate × the share where the shopper buys nothing instead (placeholder 35%; the rest substitute and the sale is not lost) × the recoverable share × a planogram complexity factor (simple ×0.85, standard ×1, complex ×1.15) × the blind-spot share, 1 − today's coverage.
  • Only the gross profit on recovered sales counts toward total value, at the margin you set.
  • Audit labour = visits per year implied by your cycle × hours per visit × loaded hourly cost, with 65% of that time modelled as replaceable by image-based checks. Placeholder.
  • Compliance value is modelled at 6% of recovered revenue, standing in for planogram and brand-visibility penalties avoided. Placeholder.
  • Coverage is the share of shelf-gap time your current audits already catch: weekly 26%, monthly 8%, quarterly 3%, ad hoc 1.5%, reduced to 40% of that for sampled field visits. With no formal audits, coverage is 1% and no audit labour is saved. Placeholder.
  • Excluded: promotional lift, supplier funding, and any change in replenishment lead time — illytical reports the gap, it does not restock the shelf.
