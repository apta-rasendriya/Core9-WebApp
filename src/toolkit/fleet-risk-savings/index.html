Assumptions behind this estimate
  • The risk score is a 0–100 planning index built from vehicle class, route profile, night-driving share, at-fault accidents per million km, and what is already fitted to the vehicles. It is not an actuarial score.
  • Accident reduction is capped at 30% and scaled by night-driving share and current on-vehicle equipment — fleets starting with nothing fitted show the largest modelled change. Placeholder industry range.
  • Insurance premium impact is modelled at up to 8% of annual premium, and only when your insurer offers telematics-linked discounts. This is off by default when Pakistani rupees are selected. Placeholder.
  • The accidents slider tops out at twice your vehicle count, and a warning shows above one at-fault accident per vehicle per year.
  • Excluded: downtime, third-party liability beyond the per-accident cost you entered, cargo loss, and any fuel or maintenance effect.
  • Currency conversion is indicative, at a fixed display rate.
