What CamCore needs from a camera stream
  • An ONVIF Profile S or RTSP stream reachable from the site network — the same stream your recorder already uses.
  • A secondary or sub-stream is preferred so analysis never competes with recording. CamCore does not alter retention or recording behaviour.
  • Analog cameras work through an existing DVR or a small encoder; the coax and camera stay in place.
  • Minimum useful resolution depends on the use case: 1080p for PPE and person detection, higher for small-object or plate work.
  • Processing happens on site, so uplink bandwidth is used for events and clips rather than continuous video.
  • Cloud platforms are rated "with notes" because stream or API access has to be confirmed per platform. Verkada Command needs review. Ring, Google Nest and Arlo generally do not expose an RTSP stream. Levels to be confirmed by Core9 engineering.
  • A brand not on this list is not a blocker — anything that speaks ONVIF or RTSP can be reviewed in a day.
