Assumptions behind this estimate
  • Labour value = hours per site per week × sites × 52 × loaded hourly cost × the automation share you set. Automation is capped at 85%: some review always stays human.
  • Incident value = incidents × cost per incident × the prevention share you set, capped at 55%. Placeholder industry range, not a Core9 measured result.
  • Audit and compliance value is modelled at 8% of the other two benefits, standing in for faster investigations and evidence retrieval. Placeholder.
  • Investment = sites × cameras per site × the per-camera annual subscription (placeholder default $1,000), unless you enter a quoted total.
  • Choosing an industry resets the incident cost and prevention share to that industry's placeholder default. You can move both afterwards.
  • Payback = investment ÷ monthly value. First-year ROI = (annual value − investment) ÷ investment.
  • Excluded: insurance effects, throughput or yield gains, and the internal time to run the programme.
  • CamCore assumes existing cameras and recorders stay in place — no hardware replacement is priced in. Confirm with the Compatibility Checker.
