What a Core9 pilot assumes
  • The pilot runs on your existing cameras, vehicles or stores — no rip-and-replace needed.
  • Duration bands: 4 weeks for small single-site scopes, 6–8 weeks for multi-site, plus a week of setup.
  • Core9 provides configuration, the edge or on-vehicle hardware for the pilot period, tuning, and the closing report. You provide site access, stream or vehicle access, and one operational owner.
  • Success criteria are agreed in writing before the pilot starts, and measured against your own baseline — not against a Core9 benchmark.
  • The pilot carries no charge. Scope, duration and exit criteria are agreed in writing up front, with no obligation to continue.
