How ready is your operation for an audit?

Ten questions on what regulators, insurers and auditors check first: accountability, evidence, data residency and monitoring. Your score updates as you answer.

  • 10 questions
  • About 3 minutes

Governance & accountability

1

Do you have a current record of every camera, dashcam and AI system in use, by site and by purpose?

2

When the system flags an event, is a named person responsible for acting on it, with a target response time?

3

For any alert, can you explain exactly why it was raised: the rule, the inputs and the threshold?

Evidence & audit

4

If an insurer or regulator asked about an incident from three months ago, how quickly could you produce the record and clip?

5

Is every alert stored with its time stamp, location, rule, severity and the person notified?

6

Can you export compliance records per site, fleet or chain for a chosen period?

Data residency & privacy

7

Is video and event data processed and stored in the country where it was captured?

8

Are retention windows set and enforced, rather than footage being kept indefinitely?

9

Is access to footage limited by role, with a log of who viewed what?

Monitoring coverage

10

How are your safety and compliance rules checked today?
